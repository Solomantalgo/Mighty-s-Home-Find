## Bed colour variant comparisons

The `color-comparisons/` folder contains 15 additional paired images: three distinct metallic bed designs, each shown in five actual frame finishes. Each landscape image has a bare frame on the left and the same design/finish fully dressed on the right. That is 30 rendered bed views in total. These are comparison plates; use the matching image for the selected model and finish. Do not tint a black bed to represent another frame colour.

The same files are copied to `public/assets/bed-color-variants/` for the website. The `README.md` in that folder lists the exact filenames and mapping.

The paired frame/dressed images are intended to demonstrate the configurator. They are separate generated renders and may not align pixel-for-pixel; use actual product photography or a layered renderer for a production-accurate live configurator.

No prices, dimensions, stock counts, delivery terms, or product specifications are established by these images. Confirm all commercial details with HomeFinds before publishing.
